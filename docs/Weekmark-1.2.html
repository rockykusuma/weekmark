Weekmark 1.2

  • Year at a Glance. See the whole year at once: all 12 months with the week number on every row, with today, the current week, holidays, milestones and sprint starts marked. Open it with the new calendar button above the widget's month, or from the menu bar. Hover a week for its dates and working days, click to pin it, ⌘C to copy it, and ← → to change the year.
  • Week numbers in any calendar. Not everyone on your team has a Mac: they can subscribe to a free calendar that shows “CW 46” every Monday in Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar. Find it at rockykusuma.github.io/weekmark/weeks.
