Week 40 is also written KW 40 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 40. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 29 Sep to Saturday 4 Oct the same number: week 40.
