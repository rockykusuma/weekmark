Week 42 is also written KW 42 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 42. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 13 Oct to Saturday 18 Oct the same number: week 42.
