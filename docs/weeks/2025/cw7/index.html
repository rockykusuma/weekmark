Week 7 is also written KW 7 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 7. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 10 Feb to Saturday 15 Feb the same number: week 7.
