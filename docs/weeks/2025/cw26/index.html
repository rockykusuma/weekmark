Week 26 is also written KW 26 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 26. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 23 Jun to Saturday 28 Jun the same number: week 26.
