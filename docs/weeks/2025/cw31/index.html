Week 31 is also written KW 31 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 31. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 28 Jul to Saturday 2 Aug the same number: week 31.
