Week 3 is also written KW 3 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 3. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 13 Jan to Saturday 18 Jan the same number: week 3.
