Week 20 is also written KW 20 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 20. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 12 May to Saturday 17 May the same number: week 20.
