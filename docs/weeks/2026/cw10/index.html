Week 10 is also written KW 10 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 10. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 2 Mar to Saturday 7 Mar the same number: week 10.
