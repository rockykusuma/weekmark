Week 53 is also written KW 53 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 53. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 28 Dec to Saturday 2 Jan a different number: week 1 of 2027.
