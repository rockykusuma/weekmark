Week 5 is also written KW 5 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 5. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 26 Jan to Saturday 31 Jan the same number: week 5.
