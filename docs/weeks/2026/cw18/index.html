Week 18 is also written KW 18 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 18. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 27 Apr to Saturday 2 May the same number: week 18.
