Week 1 is also written KW 1 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 1. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 29 Dec to Saturday 3 Jan the same number: week 1.
