Week 6 is also written KW 6 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 6. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 2 Feb to Saturday 7 Feb the same number: week 6.
