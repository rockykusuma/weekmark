Week 43 is also written KW 43 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 43. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 19 Oct to Saturday 24 Oct the same number: week 43.
