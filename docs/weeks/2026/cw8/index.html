Week 8 is also written KW 8 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 8. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 16 Feb to Saturday 21 Feb the same number: week 8.
