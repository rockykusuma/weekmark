Week 28 is also written KW 28 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 28. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 6 Jul to Saturday 11 Jul the same number: week 28.
