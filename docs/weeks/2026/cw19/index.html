Week 19 is also written KW 19 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 19. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 4 May to Saturday 9 May the same number: week 19.
