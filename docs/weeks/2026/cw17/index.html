Week 17 is also written KW 17 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 17. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 20 Apr to Saturday 25 Apr the same number: week 17.
