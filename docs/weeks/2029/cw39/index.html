Week 39 is also written KW 39 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 39. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 24 Sep to Saturday 29 Sep the same number: week 39.
