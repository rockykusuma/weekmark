Week 2 is also written KW 2 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 2. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 8 Jan to Saturday 13 Jan the same number: week 2.
