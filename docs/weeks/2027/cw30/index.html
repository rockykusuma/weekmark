Week 30 is also written KW 30 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 30. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 26 Jul to Saturday 31 Jul a different number: week 31.
