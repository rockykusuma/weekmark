Week 9 is also written KW 9 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 9. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 1 Mar to Saturday 6 Mar a different number: week 10.
