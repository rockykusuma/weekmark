Week 22 is also written KW 22 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 22. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 31 May to Saturday 5 Jun a different number: week 23.
