Week 21 is also written KW 21 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 21. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 24 May to Saturday 29 May a different number: week 22.
