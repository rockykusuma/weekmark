Week 48 is also written KW 48 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 48. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 29 Nov to Saturday 4 Dec a different number: week 49.
