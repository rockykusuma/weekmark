Week 4 is also written KW 4 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 4. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 25 Jan to Saturday 30 Jan a different number: week 5.
