Week 12 is also written KW 12 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 12. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 22 Mar to Saturday 27 Mar a different number: week 13.
