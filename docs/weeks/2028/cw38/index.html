Week 38 is also written KW 38 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 38. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 18 Sep to Saturday 23 Sep a different number: week 39.
