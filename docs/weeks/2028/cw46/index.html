Week 46 is also written KW 46 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 46. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 13 Nov to Saturday 18 Nov a different number: week 47.
