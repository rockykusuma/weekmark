Week 40 is also written KW 40 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 40. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 2 Oct to Saturday 7 Oct a different number: week 41.
