Week 41 is also written KW 41 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 41. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 9 Oct to Saturday 14 Oct a different number: week 42.
