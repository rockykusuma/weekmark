Week 27 is also written KW 27 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 27. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 3 Jul to Saturday 8 Jul a different number: week 28.
