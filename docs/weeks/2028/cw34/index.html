Week 34 is also written KW 34 (Kalenderwoche) or CW 34. ISO weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year’s first Thursday. US calendars start weeks on Sunday and count the week with 1 January as week 1, so they give Monday 21 Aug to Saturday 26 Aug a different number: week 35.
