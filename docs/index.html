On your desktop

Made for people who plan in weeks

Release trains, sprint reviews, “we’ll ship it in CW46”. Weekmark keeps that vocabulary on screen and does the date maths for you.

⌃⌥W

Quick Finder

Look up a week, date, range or countdown from any app. Press ⏎ to copy “CW46 (9–15 Nov)” into Slack or Jira.

MILESTONES

Countdowns that count working days

Pin “Release 5.0 → CW46” and see 5 weeks · 30 working days left. Milestone weeks are flagged in the calendar.

SPRINTS · PI · Q

Your team’s cadence

Sprint 20 · day 5/9, PI 4 · sprint 5/5, Q3 FY27. Set the sprint length once and the app counts forward.

HOLIDAYS

Working days that are actually right

Public holidays for 10 countries, plus your company’s holiday calendar imported from .ics.

ISO ≠ US

Catches the off-by-one

In some years ISO and US week numbers differ. Weekmark tells you when “CW10” means CW11 to your US colleagues.

MULTI-DISPLAY

Lives on your second screen

Sits on the desktop behind your windows, remembers which display it belongs to, and returns when you plug it back in.
