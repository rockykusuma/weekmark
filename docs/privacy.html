Weekmark for Mac, Windows, iPhone and Apple Watch

Privacy policy

Weekmark collects no data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your settings, milestones or anything you type.

What stays on your devices

  • Your settings, milestones, sprints, quarters and holiday choices are stored on your device.
  • A company holiday calendar (.ics) you import is read on your device and is never uploaded.
  • What you type into the search field or Quick Finder is worked out on your device and is never sent anywhere.

iCloud sync (Apple devices)

On Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch, Weekmark can keep your milestones and planning settings in step through your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account; we cannot see or access it. You can turn iCloud off for Weekmark in your device’s settings.

Network access

  • The Mac app checks for updates by downloading a small file from this website (hosted by GitHub Pages). Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address; Weekmark sends nothing else.
  • The Windows app checks for updates once a day. It asks GitHub’s releases API about this project’s releases and downloads new versions from GitHub. GitHub can see your IP address; Weekmark sends nothing else. You can turn this off in Settings → General → Updates.
  • The iPhone and Apple Watch apps make no network requests of their own.

Children

Weekmark collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
