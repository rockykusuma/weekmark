The fourth test build of Weekmark for Windows. This is a pre-release for colleagues to try. The Mac app's download is unaffected.

If you have 0.3.0 installed, this one arrives by itself: the menu shows **Restart to Update to 0.3.1** within a day,
or press **Check Now** in Settings → General → Updates. Please tell us whether that worked: it's the first real test
of automatic updates.

## What's new

- **Less memory with the Solid and Clear styles.** Weekmark now draws these without the graphics card, using about
  30 MB less memory. The saving starts the next time Weekmark starts. The Glass style needs the graphics card and works as before. Switching to Glass takes effect
  when you quit and reopen Weekmark; Settings says so.
- **Last checked** in Settings → General → Updates shows the date once the check was before today.
- **Check for Updates…** scrolls Settings to the whole Updates section, not just its title.
- The tray icon no longer blinks when Restart to Update has nothing to install.

Setup, Portable, x64 versus ARM64 and the unsigned-build warning work as in 0.1.0: see the
[0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/rockykusuma/weekmark/blob/windows-v0.1.0/Windows/release-notes/0.1.0.md).

## Please test

Work through the [test checklist](https://github.com/rockykusuma/weekmark/blob/windows-v0.3.1/Windows/TESTING.md),
especially items 20 (updates) and 21 (memory and style), and report what you find as a
