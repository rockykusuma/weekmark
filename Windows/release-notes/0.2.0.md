The second test build of Weekmark for Windows. This is a pre-release for colleagues to try. The Mac app's download is unaffected.

## What's new

- **Right-click the widget** for the app menu, even when it sits on the desktop behind your windows.
- **Add Milestone in CW…**: right-click a week in the month grid and Settings opens with that week filled in and the
  name ready to type.
- **Weeks in YYYY** in the tray menu and the widget menu: pick any week of the year and the widget comes forward on that month.
- **Holidays by hand:** in Settings → Holidays add your own holiday with a name and date, **Remove All** the company
  holidays, and see the next six under **Coming up**.
- **Show Welcome Screen Again** in Settings → General.

Setup, Portable, x64 versus ARM64 and the unsigned-build warning work as in 0.1.0: see the
[0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/rockykusuma/weekmark/blob/windows-v0.1.0/Windows/release-notes/0.1.0.md).

## Please test

Work through the [test checklist](https://github.com/rockykusuma/weekmark/blob/windows-v0.2.0/Windows/TESTING.md),
especially item 18, and report what you find as a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/rockykusuma/weekmark/issues),
including your Windows version, whether your PC is x64 or ARM64, and which checklist items failed.
