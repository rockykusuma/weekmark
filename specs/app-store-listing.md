## Description [4000]
Weekmark shows the calendar week number wherever you look: on the Lock Screen, in Home Screen and StandBy widgets, and on your Apple Watch. It’s made for people who plan in weeks — release trains, sprint reviews, “we’ll ship it in CW46”.

THE WEEK, EVERYWHERE
• “CW44” above the clock on the Lock Screen, or as a ring that fills through the week
• Small, medium and large widgets with the week strip, the next milestone and a month grid with week numbers
• Apple Watch app and complications

LOOK UP ANY WEEK
Type a week, a date, an offset, a range or a countdown:
• 46 or CW46 → the dates, how far away, working days and holidays
• Dec 12 → the week it falls in
• +6w → the week six weeks from now
• 46-50 → how many weeks and working days
• until CW52 → weeks and working days left

THE WHOLE YEAR
See all 12 months with every week number on one screen, and step through the weeks without losing your place.

YOUR TEAM’S CADENCE
• Milestones with countdowns in weeks and working days
• Sprints, PIs and fiscal quarters, counted forward automatically
• Public holidays for 10 countries, so working-day counts are right
• ISO 8601 or US week numbering

WORKS WITH THE MAC APP
Use Weekmark on your Mac too? Your milestones and planning settings sync through your own iCloud.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

## Review notes [4000]
Paste into App Review Information → Notes, and into the reply to App Review. Attach the screen recording to both.

1. Screen recording
Attached: recorded on an iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts at app launch and shows the Week, Find, Year and Settings tabs, adding a milestone, and the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Weekmark has no account registration, login, user-generated content, or paid content.

2. Purpose and audience
Weekmark shows the calendar week number ("CW44") in the app, in Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets, and on Apple Watch. Many workplaces, especially in Europe and in software teams, plan in week numbers ("we ship in CW46"). iOS doesn't show the week number anywhere at a glance, so people search for "what week is it". The audience is people who plan in weeks: software teams, project and product managers, release managers, and anyone working with colleagues who use week numbers. The app also converts between weeks and dates, counts weeks and working days to a milestone (taking public holidays into account), and keeps track of sprints and fiscal quarters.

3. Setup and access
No account, login, or sample files are needed. Everything works offline.
- Launch the app. The Week tab shows the current week, with arrows to step through weeks.
- Find tab: type 46, Dec 12, +6w, 46-50 or until CW52.
- Year tab: all 12 months with week numbers.
- Settings: week numbering (ISO 8601 or US), holiday country, milestones, sprints and fiscal quarters.
- Widgets: long-press the Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → Weekmark. For the Lock Screen: long-press it → Customize → Lock Screen → add a widget.
- Apple Watch: install from the Watch app, then add a Weekmark complication to a watch face.
- Optional: Settings → Holidays → Import .ics File accepts any calendar file exported from a calendar app (for example, a company holiday list).

4. External services
None. Weekmark has no servers, no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no ads and no AI services, and it makes no network requests of its own. Holiday dates are calculated on the device. The only Apple service it uses is iCloud key-value storage, to optionally sync the user's own milestones and settings between their iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac. Users can turn this off in iOS Settings. Data Not Collected.

5. Regional differences
The app works the same in all regions. On first launch it picks a holiday country from the device region if it is one of the 10 built-in countries (US, UK, Germany, Denmark, Netherlands, France, Sweden, Norway, Poland, India). Users can change this and the week numbering at any time. The app is in English.

6. Regulated industry or third-party material
Not applicable. Weekmark is a calendar utility; it uses no protected third-party material, and public holiday dates are public information.
